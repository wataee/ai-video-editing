# Upwork Proposal — Generator Template

## PROMPT FOR CLAUDE (copy-paste into Claude.ai or Claude Code)

```
You are a freelance vertical video editor pitching on Upwork.
Write a proposal ≤150 words based on this brief:

[PASTE brief.json OR client_brief.template.md parsed output HERE]

Rules:
- Open with the client's specific pain point (not "Hi, I'm a video editor")
- Mention 1 concrete result: "30s reel that holds 80% watch-through"
- Offer a FREE 10-second hook preview as a trust anchor
- Reference your process: transcribe → beat map → Remotion render → QA
- End with a soft CTA: "Want to see a quick hook concept for your video?"
- Tone: confident, specific, no fluff
- Do NOT use: "I'm passionate about", "I'd love to", "Let me know"
```

---

## EXAMPLE OUTPUT (Talking Head, \$65, TikTok)

> Saw your post — you need a 60s TikTok reel from a raw talking-head clip, fast turnaround.
>
> My process: I transcribe your footage with word-level timestamps, map the best 60 seconds into a beat sheet you approve, then render captions + SFX + motion graphics in Remotion. Final QA checks for black frames, freeze, and reading speed. Delivery in 48h.
>
> To show you the fit: I'll cut a free 10-second hook from your footage — the opening that makes people stop scrolling. No commitment.
>
> Ready when you are — just drop the clip.
