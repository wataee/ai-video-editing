# 10. The 6 Visual Hook Archetypes (0-3s Retention)

## 1. Archetype 1: The Counter-Intuitive Claim
- **Visual Trigger**: Giant central high-contrast headline (`Montserrat-Black`) with red accent word. Camera executes immediate `push-in` (1.0 -> 1.08) in first 15 frames.
- **Graphic**: Negative icon badge or strikethrough effect.
- **Sound**: Heavy low impact hit (`impacts/heavy-cinematic-hit.wav`) on frame 0, followed by subtle air whoosh.
- **Script Example**: "Everything you've been told about building wealth is backwards."

## 2. Archetype 2: The Rapid Zoom Question
- **Visual Trigger**: Speaker starts at wide shot, immediately punch-zooms (scale 1.15) on frame 1 with subtle camera shake.
- **Graphic**: Two large question marks or glowing yellow accent brackets framing speaker's face.
- **Sound**: Fast whip-whoosh (`whooshes/fast-swish-air.wav`) into muted sub bass.
- **Script Example**: "Why is nobody talking about what happened to the housing market yesterday?"

## 3. Archetype 3: The Data Anomaly (Chart/Ticker)
- **Visual Trigger**: Dark `vox-editorial-data` grid. Giant `NumberTicker` counting from 0 to a shocking statistic with rapid tick audio. Bar chart shoots upward with spring bounce.
- **Graphic**: Bright canary yellow (`#FFD60A`) line or bar soaring off the chart scale.
- **Sound**: High-speed digital click sequence (`sci-fi/digital-tick-single.wav`) resolving into a resonant bell/ding.
- **Script Example**: "This single business metric dropped by 94% in less than 48 hours."

## 4. Archetype 4: The Screen Grab Reality (The Receipt)
- **Visual Trigger**: Realistic mobile screenshot or email window (`LiquidGlassCard`) floating at a 4-degree isometric tilt. Camera executes gentle `push-out` (1.06 -> 1.0).
- **Graphic**: Glowing yellow highlighter stroke brushing across the critical sentence.
- **Sound**: Crisp paper slide (`textures/paper-slide-light.wav`) + digital shutter click.
- **Script Example**: "I wasn't supposed to show anyone this internal email."

## 5. Archetype 5: The Extreme Negative Warning
- **Visual Trigger**: High-contrast flashing badge "STOP DOING THIS" or hazard tape motif. Monochromatic grayscale footage with glowing neon red warning box.
- **Graphic**: Warning icon bouncing into place using `spring({ damping: 10, stiffness: 140 })`.
- **Sound**: Sub drop rumble (`risers/sub-drop-short.wav`) + harsh mechanical thud.
- **Script Example**: "If you make this one mistake on your tax return, you could lose everything."

## 6. Archetype 6: The Split Reality (Before vs. After / Amateur vs. Pro)
- **Visual Trigger**: Split-screen line slicing vertically down the center at frame 10. Left side desaturated, right side vibrant high-contrast 4K grade.
- **Graphic**: "99% of people" (left) vs "Top 1%" (right) label pills.
- **Sound**: Fast laser/knife pass whoosh (`whooshes/transitional-air-pass.wav`) as split line descends.
- **Script Example**: "Here is the difference between an amateur editor and someone who makes $10,000 a month."
