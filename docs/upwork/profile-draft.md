## Profile Overview

```markdown
Are your viewers dropping off in the first 3 seconds, or does your editing feel like the same recycled CapCut template everyone else is using?

I help founders, podcasters, and expert creators turn long-form recordings and talking-head footage into high-retention 9:16 vertical videos (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) with a clean, broadcast-standard aesthetic.

### What I Focus On:
1. **The First 3 Seconds (Hook Architecture):** Bold typography within safe zones, semantic punchwords, and subtle auditory cues designed to stop the scroll.
2. **Tight, Natural Pacing:** Removing filler pauses and dead air while preserving the natural cadence and breathing of your speech.
3. **Proof-Driven Visuals:** Real artifacts instead of cheap stock video — verified screenshots, data charts, and document highlights that build audience authority.
4. **Broadcast Audio & Safe Zones:** Speech normalized to -14 LUFS, SFX calibrated relative to speech (-14 to -20 dB), and zero UI overlaps on TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube Shorts.

### How I Use Automation (Transparent & Local):
I leverage a proprietary local technical pipeline built on Remotion (code-driven parametric motion) and faster-whisper (local word-level transcription). This means:
- Subtitle synchronization is calculated with frame-level accuracy.
- Layouts and motion physics are mathematically smooth (no clunky transitions or visual artifacts).
- Automated 10 FPS QA audits eliminate black frames, frozen screens, and subtitle typos before you ever see a draft.
- Every creative decision — B-roll selection, visual proof, pacing, and color palette — is curated and reviewed by hand.

### Confidentiality & Local Processing (NDA Compliant):
All client footage, unreleased podcast episodes, and proprietary materials are processed and rendered strictly on an encrypted local workstation. Your raw assets are never uploaded to public AI training clouds or third-party web tools. Strict NDA compliance guaranteed.
```
